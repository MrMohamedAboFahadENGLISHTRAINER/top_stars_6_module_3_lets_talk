4. Grammar Focus: LOOK! Box (Page 35)

Present Perfect Continuous vs. Present Perfect (*for* vs *since*).

Action in Progress

Present Perfect Continuous

have / has + been + verb(-ing)

• Bob has been sending emails since 9 o'clock.

• I have been living in California for 6 months.

Completed Result

Present Perfect Simple

have / has + Past Participle (V3)

• He has sent 5 emails so far.

• I've visited the ancient city of Cobá.

Practice: Choose 'for' or 'since'

1. Steve has been living in California _____ six months.
2. Bob has been sending emails _____ nine o'clock.
3. I have been studying Spanish _____ May.
